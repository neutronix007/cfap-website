HMRC Enquiries & Compliance Checks

HMRC may open a compliance check where it wishes to verify the accuracy of a tax return or understand a particular tax position. These enquiries can arise from routine risk assessment, third-party information, disclosure campaigns or specific technical issues.

We provide representation throughout the enquiry process, including:

  1. Corporation Tax enquiries
  2. Self Assessment enquiries
  3. VAT compliance checks
  4. Employer compliance reviews
  5. R&D tax relief compliance checks
  6. Capital allowance enquiries
  7. HMRC information requests

We review the technical issues raised, prepare responses to HMRC and support clients throughout the enquiry until the matter is concluded.

HMRC Information Notices

Where HMRC issues a formal information notice, it is important to understand both your obligations and your rights.

We advise clients on responding to information requests, reviewing the scope of notices issued under Schedule 36 Finance Act 2008 and preparing appropriate responses supported by relevant documentation.

Tax Appeals & Penalty Reviews

If you disagree with an HMRC decision, you may have statutory rights of appeal.

We advise on:

  1. Late filing penalties
  2. Late payment penalties
  3. Inaccuracy penalties
  4. Reasonable excuse claims
  5. Statutory Reviews
  6. Alternative Dispute Resolution (ADR), where appropriate
  7. Appeals against HMRC decisions

Where appropriate, we prepare technical representations supported by legislation, HMRC guidance and relevant case law.

Voluntary Disclosures

Where historic tax errors are identified, voluntary disclosure can often provide a structured route to correcting matters before HMRC takes further action.

We assist businesses and individuals in reviewing historic tax positions, quantifying any adjustments required and preparing disclosures in accordance with HMRC procedures.

Technical Tax Disputes

Not every dispute concerns whether tax is due. Many arise because of differing interpretations of tax legislation or the application of reliefs.

We advise on disputes involving:

  1. Corporation Tax
  2. Capital Allowances
  3. Research & Development (RDEC)
  4. Transfer Pricing
  5. Corporate Interest Restriction (CIR)
  6. Patent Box
  7. Share schemes and employment-related securities
  8. Business restructures
  9. Residence and permanent establishment issues

Where specialist technical analysis is required, we work with clients and their existing advisers to present well-supported tax positions.
