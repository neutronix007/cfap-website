About us

Independent specialists in HMRC disputes

Established in 2022, we are an independent UK tax practice dedicated to HMRC disputes and investigations.

Our work centres on representing clients during HMRC enquiries, reviewing complex tax positions and advising on disputes involving UK direct taxes. We work alongside accountants and legal advisers where additional technical expertise is required, providing independent advice that complements their existing services.

We believe clients should have access to clear, practical guidance when dealing with HMRC. Every matter is approached with careful technical analysis, open communication and a commitment to resolving issues professionally and efficiently.

Meet the Founder

Specialist support during HMRC enquiries and tax disputes

I established the practice after several years working in UK tax and accountancy, advising businesses on Corporation Tax compliance and technical tax matters.

The firm was founded to provide businesses with specialist support during HMRC enquiries and tax disputes. My focus is on helping clients understand the technical issues involved, assess their options and respond to HMRC with confidence.

I work closely with business owners, directors and professional advisers to deliver practical, commercially aware tax advice grounded in current UK tax legislation.
